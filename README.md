## 👋 About Me

I'm **Qinglin Yang (杨青霖)**, currently a Master's student in **Computer Technology** at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/).

I received my B.E. degree from [China University of Geosciences (Wuhan)](https://www.cug.edu.cn/), where my undergraduate research focused on **Remote Sensing Time-Series Analysis** and **Deep Learning–based Change Detection**, particularly long-term mangrove ecosystem monitoring using dense Landsat imagery.

My current research focuses on **Mobile GUI Agents**, **Agent Harness**, and **LLM Post-training**, with an interest in building more capable, efficient, and reliable autonomous agents.
